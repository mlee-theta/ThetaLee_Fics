Everybody Leaves

"So you lied."

 

It was after the party, after everyone had cleaned up and gone home. Buck hadn't even waited that long, had bounced within the hour of Eddie's impromptu announcement. He hadn't been feeling the festive cheer before, and it was definitely dead afterwards. At first, Buck wasn't even angry. Buck was just... Cold. Empty. Numb. Belatedly, he realized he hadn't even said goodbye to anyone. Not to Bobby or Athena, to Hen or Ravi, to any of the kids. He hadn't even said goodbye to Taylor. Or Eddie.

 

Fucking Eddie.

 

It was hours later, the sun had set and his phone had been turned off hours ago because he just didn't have it in him to fake anything. He had just been too numb. But now? Now he was angry.

 

So here he was, middle of the night, at Eddie's door. Here he was, middle of the night, staring his best friend in the face and feeling every ounce of that anger burn away the cold and the numbness from earlier.

 

"What are you talking about, Buck?"

 

"You lied," Buck repeated, jabbing his finger into the other man's chest. "That won't be us, Buck. You're stuck with us, Buck."

 

"Man, what are you-" Eddie leaned against the doorway. "Is this about me leaving the 118?"

 

"Of course it's about you leaving!"

 

"What do you expect me to do, Buck? Chris is worried about losing another parent."

 

"And what, you think you're safer doing this?" Buck asked. "Like I didn't nearly die eating bread? Like Chris and I didn't nearly die having a day on the pier? Like that guy yesterday didn't nearly die backing into his driveway?"

 

"I just want to be safe for my kid."

 

"Then don't cut your fucking safety line!" Buck snapped. "You're in just as much danger of dying crossing the street as you are riding the ladder truck, I think Shannon proved that!"

 

And that was... Too far. Much too far. But he was too angry to be sorry.

 

"You didn't even talk to me about it first! I'm supposed to have your back."

 

"You were too busy worrying about Taylor," Eddie said, every word dripping with acid.

 

"At least Taylor is there for me to worry about!" Buck shot back. "Taylor is staying! That's more than I can say about you. Maddie left, Chimney left, everyone is leaving, just like I was afraid would happen. And you said it wouldn't, you promised it wouldn't, and now you're leaving too."

 

"Are you here just to yell at me?" Eddie asked, crossing his arms, shoulders stiff.

 

"No, I'm here to give you this back." He reached into his pocket and thrust the small piece of metal at Eddie, who fumbled for it awkwardly.

 

"Your key?" he asked, staring at it. "Buck-"

 

"I am tired of people leaving. I am done with people leaving. So I'm leaving."

 

"But- What about Christopher?"

 

"He can call me when he wants to talk, until he decides to leave, too," Buck said, turning to walk away.

 

"Buck- Evan, don't-"

 

"I'm done, Eddie!" he shouted, getting into the jeep. He slammed the door shut, started the engine. "I'm done."

 

He had locks to change, a girlfriend to apologize to, a broken family to look forward to.

 

Buck was so done, with everything.
